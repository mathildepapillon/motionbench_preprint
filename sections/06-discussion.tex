\section{Limitations and Conclusions}
\label{sec:discussion}
% =====================================================================

MotionBench-XAI exposes and compares the design choices behind game-theoretic explanations for spatiotemporal machine learning. The benchmark grades ten attribution methods against exact ground truths on five designed datasets, runs the same pipeline on three real ones, and distills the outcome into useful insights. We conclude with two limitations that offer promising avenues for future work. First, the oracle construction is limited to Gaussian-field and copula families: extending exact ground truths to richer designed manifolds, such as articulated dynamics and discrete events, would widen the benchmark's reach. Second, the suite's dimensions are moderate ($J \leq 17$, $M \leq 68$) and its real-data conclusions are specific to the evaluated checkpoints: scaling to finer granularities and more architectures could better characterize real-world explanation scenarios. Overall, we look forward to MotionBench-XAI powering more intentional and practical use of Shapley explanation in applied spatiotemporal settings.
